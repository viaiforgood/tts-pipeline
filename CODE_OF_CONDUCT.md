# Code of Conduct

We want `tts-pipeline` to be a respectful, useful, and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarity when needed.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy, especially around user text, audio, generated media, and credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, private transcripts, or generated private media.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, decline contributions, or block participants who do not follow this code of conduct.
